# Brainstorm report: October 7, 2026

## 1. How it started: the megametroidvania
You started with **"a combo of Mega Man and a metroidvania,"** which you called a *megametroidvania*.

**Existing examples we looked at:** *Mega Man ZX / ZX Advent*, which is the closest match; *Gravity Circuit*; and *Hollow Knight*, *Axiom Verge* and *Blasphemous* as models for ability-gated exploration.

## 2. How the combat system developed
| Step | Idea | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Helldivers 2-style enemy types (light, medium, heavy, tank, air) | Simplified to infantry, armored and air |
| 2 | Primary, secondary and tertiary weapons, plus armor with stat allocation | Felt too complicated |
| 3 | Dash as an anti-armor tool | You reversed it: dash and melee for infantry, buster for armored, an alt buster for air |
| 4 | Mega Man's shots already hit fliers | Infantry and air merged into one class |
| 5 | **Final: three armor tiers** | Weak (anything works), medium (buster or charge), heavy (charge only) |

**Moves that were added:** wall jump, and a dash that does damage.

**Progression:**
- Each move gets its own skill tree
- Enemies scale up, so a late weak enemy is as tough as an early heavy one, but you've grown too
- **Upgrades let moves break higher armor tiers.** This is the progression itself: for example, upgraded melee eventually cuts through medium armor.

**Identity:** Mega Man movement and shooting, a touch of beat-'em-up (dash and melee), and metroidvania complexity (hidden items and rooms).

## 3. Structure and influences
- **Bosses don't give powers.** They drop items that NPCs want back, and the NPCs reward you with skill points or upgrades. Your example was returning an item to a mage.
- **Contra influence:** run-and-gun intensity, more enemies on screen, set pieces, and **perspective-changing levels**. We decided against Contra's one-hit deaths.
- **Vehicle sections:** horizontal or vertical scrolling-shooter segments. You want the vehicle to be **Blade Runner-style** (a spinner, rain and neon).
- **Setting:** you moved from science-fantasy (the mage was only an example) to **sci-fi and cyberpunk tech**.

## 4. Side ideas that came up and were set aside

**Army of One (your story file):** Marcus "Shadow" Williams, the Order of Hermes, "one alone, many tools," and drone swarms that mirror animals (falcon, dog, horse).
- You noted parts of it were AI-generated, and you're separating your own ideas from the AI's.
- Ideas we considered borrowing: "one alone, many tools," **jamming that forces melee**, and PMC contract missions.
- **Decision:** it stays a story or movie.

**The eras game:** the same game across eras, with companions changing form (falcon to air drone to AI swarm, dog to ground drone to cyber-hound, horse to car to spinner), possibly through a bloodline.
- **Decision:** parked as its own separate idea.

## 5. The realization: too many games in one
You noticed you were mixing ideas that belong in separate games. When the Agent notes added **stealth and scanning**, you saw that "think before you act" conflicts with Mega Man-style power growth and beat-'em-up action.

**Final split:**
| Game | Focus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Megametroidvania** | Action, power growth, beat-'em-up plus shooter | Has its own folder: `Projects\Action Game` |
| **Agent Game** | Stealth and tactics: scan, plan, execute | Has its own folder: `Projects\Agent Game` |
| **Eras game** | Bloodline across time periods | Parked |
| **Army of One** | Story or movie | Stays a story |

## 6. Agent Game: current state
**Source:** your 2017 notes (`Projects\Video Games\agent.txt`), using the version **without** the experiment and mutation story.

**World:** a high-tech near future with AI, drones, androids, exo-suits, privacy bubbles, see-through-wall WiFi, EM-spectrum glasses, BCI, genetic engineering, cloning, thought recording and a human body shop.

**Gear:** a badge (AI, communicator, scanner), a weapon (weak-to-strong energy beam, shield or disruptor, self-destruct), a drone, and a surface vehicle.

**Mechanics ideas:**
- **Shared modules** (AI, scanner, beam, shield, self-destruct, stealth) as the upgrade system
- The scanner and vision modes reveal enemies, weak points, hidden paths and armor tiers
- Fragile, lethal combat: going loud is possible but risky
- Upgrades expand your *options* more than raw stats
- **Inspirations:** *Mark of the Ninja*, *Katana Zero*, *Hotline Miami*, *Dishonored*, *Invisible Inc.*, *Deus Ex*, *Gunpoint*

**Visual style (decided):** **2D pixel art with mixed perspectives**
- Top-down for markets, streets and hubs
- Side view for building infiltration, towers and vents
